## 2. Children's Privacy Policy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Little Blueprint is deeply committed to protecting the privacy of children and families. We adhere strictly to the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** in the United States and the **General Data Protection Regulation - Kids (GDPR-K)** in the European Union and UK.

### A. Who Operates the Account?
* **Parents Only:** The Little Blueprint application and web quiz are designed exclusively for parents, legal guardians, and adult gift-givers aged 18 and older. Children under the age of 13 are **never permitted to create accounts or interact directly with the app**.
* All data entry regarding a child is performed exclusively by the verified adult parent or guardian.

### B. What Child Data Is Collected?
To perform our astronomical calculations and generate developmental blueprints, we collect:
1. Child's First Name or Nickname (Pseudonyms fully supported).
2. Date of Birth.
3. Exact Time of Birth.
4. City / Geolocation of Birth (used strictly to obtain astronomical latitude/longitude coordinates).

### C. What Child Data Is NEVER Collected?
* We **never** collect, request, or store:
  * Photographs or videos of children.
  * Real-time GPS location telemetry or tracking.
  * Medical records, biometric data, or school grading records.
  * Audio recordings of children.

### D. Data Encryption & Zero Third-Party Monetization
* All child profile records are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
* **We do not sell, rent, or monetize child data to data brokers, advertising networks, or third-party behavioral trackers.** Data is used exclusively within the app to render the child's blueprint and parent-child synastry.
* **Right to Erasure (Forget Me):** Parents may permanently delete their account and all associated child birth data at any time via a single tap in the App Settings or by contacting `privacy@littleblueprint.com`.
