## 2. In-Depth Feature Specifications

### Module 1: The Inborn Child Blueprint (Natal Profile)
* **User Story:** As an exhausted parent, I want to understand my child's sensory and emotional wiring so I stop blaming myself or forcing methods that don't fit them.
* **Core Screens & Components:**
  1. **The Inborn Archetype Badge:** Displays the child's core archetype (e.g., *"The Kinetic Pioneer"*, *"The Sensitive Sage"*).
  2. **Sensory Regulation Card:**
     * *Sensory Triggers:* What overwhelms them (synthetic fabrics, sudden noise, visual clutter).
     * *Soothing Mechanisms:* How they reset their nervous system (deep pressure, solitary play, warm water).
  3. **Learning Modality Map:** Details whether they are a Visual, Tactile-Kinesthetic, Verbal, or Intuitive learner.
  4. **The "Do Not Do" Warning:** A protective list of discipline tactics that actively backfire on this child's temperament.

---

### Module 2: The Parent-Child Harmony Engine (Synastry)
* **User Story:** As a mother who feels guilty for losing my patience, I want to understand why my child's behavior triggers me so I can respond calmly.
* **Core Screens & Components:**
  1. **The Dynamic Harmony Score (0–100):** Visual elemental pairing (e.g., *Air Mother + Water Child*).
  2. **The Trigger Mirror:** Explains the neurological friction point (e.g., *"When you speak quickly, your child's nervous system experiences it as a threat, shutting down their listening ears"*).
  3. **The Emergency Crisis Card (3-Step Script):**
     * *Step 1 (Body Pause):* What the parent must physically do (drop to eye level, exhale).
     * *Step 2 (The Script):* Exact words to say (*"I see you are having big feelings. I am here while you reset."*).
     * *Step 3 (The Physical Anchor):* Somatic touch or space to give.

---

### Module 3: Daily Somatic Weather & Meltdown Forecast
* **User Story:** As a busy parent planning my day, I want to know when my child will be most vulnerable to overstimulation so I can avoid public meltdowns.
* **Core Screens & Components:**
  1. **Today's Sensory Alignment Score (0–100):** Visual circular gauge on the home screen.
  2. **Stimulation Windows:**
     * *Green Window (Peak Social & Exploration Hours):* Best time for playground, grocery shopping, or preschool drop-off.
     * *Amber/Red Window (Overstimulation Caution):* High risk of sensory overwhelm; prioritize quiet home activities.
  3. **The Daily 2-Minute Grounding Ritual:** One bite-sized, evening micro-connection ritual (e.g., *lavender foot massage, reading by candlelight, tactile playdough reset*).

### Module 4: "The Doula" AI Pediatric Companion
* **User Story:** As an anxious parent in the middle of a bedtime battle, I want immediate personalized advice without reading a 300-page book.
* **Prompt Architecture:**
  * Uses Google Gemini 1.5 Pro with prompt-caching containing:
    1. Child's full natal coordinates, Nakshatra, and elemental strengths.
    2. Parent's natal coordinates and cognitive pace.
    3. Evidence-based developmental frameworks (Janet Lansbury, Dr. Becky Kennedy, Montessori, sensory integration).
  * **Tone Rules:** Warm, maternal, non-judgmental, actionable in 3 bullet points or less.
